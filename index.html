How to Play 2048

  • Move the tiles — swipe or press an arrow key to slide every tile in that direction.
  • Merge matching numbers — when two tiles with the same number touch, they combine into one tile worth double.
  • Reach 2048 — keep merging until you create the 2048 tile. You win, but the game continues.
  • Don't fill the board — the game ends when the grid is full and no moves remain.
